Before the storm

Get ready before the next one.

Storm season on the Coffs Coast runs through spring and summer. These are worth sorting while the sky’s still blue.

Surge protection

A surge protection device fitted at the switchboard helps protect TVs, computers, appliances and aircon from power surges during storms and when the power comes back on.

Generator inlet

A generator inlet and changeover switch lets you run the house — or the circuits that matter — from a portable generator during a long outage, safely and legally.

Safety switch check

Safety switches tested, and older boards upgraded so every circuit is protected. They’re what cuts the power fast when water gets where it shouldn’t.

Running a generator?

Never plug it into a powerpoint.

Back-feeding a generator through a powerpoint with a double-ended lead can send power back out into the street and kill a line worker fixing the outage. It can also damage your wiring and the generator.

The safe way is a generator inlet with a changeover switch, installed by a licensed electrician.

Common questions

Storms and your power.

Who do I call if powerlines are down?

Essential Energy on 13 20 80 — and 000 if anyone is in danger. Stay at least 8 metres away and keep others back. Once the network side is safe, Mac can sort anything on your side of the meter.

The power came back on but something’s not working. Is that you?

Yes. Dead circuits, a safety switch that won’t reset, or appliances that stopped after the storm are all worth a call.

Will surge protection stop lightning damage?

It helps a lot with surges from storms and the power coming back on, which is where most damage comes from. Nothing gives full protection against a direct lightning strike, so it’s still worth unplugging expensive gear when a big storm is close.

Can I just run my generator through a powerpoint?

No. It’s illegal and dangerous — it can back-feed into the network. A generator inlet with a changeover switch is the safe way to do it.

Can you write a report for my insurance company?

Yes. Mac can inspect the damage and provide a written report for your claim.
