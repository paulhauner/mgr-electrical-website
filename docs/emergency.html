Call straight away for

Don’t wait on these.

A burning smell, or something hot

A hot switch, powerpoint or fitting, or any smell of burning plastic. Turn the circuit off at the board if you safely can, then ring.

Total or partial power loss

Especially if the neighbours still have power — that usually means the fault is on your side of the meter.

A breaker that won’t stay on

A safety switch tripping repeatedly is doing its job. Something is causing it, and it needs finding rather than resetting.

Storm or water damage

Water anywhere near switchboards, wiring or fittings. Stay clear of it and ring before touching anything.

While you wait

A few things that help.

If it’s safe to do so, these make the callout faster — but never go poking around inside a switchboard.

  • Switch off the circuit at the board if you can do it safely
  • Unplug appliances on the affected circuit
  • Check whether neighbours have power too
  • Keep everyone clear of any water near wiring
