Repairs & servicing

Aircon playing up? Mac will sort it.

Most aircon faults are either the unit or the power to it. Mac is a licensed electrician, so he can fix both in the one visit — no second trade, no second call-out.

Repairs

Not cooling like it used to, leaking, rattling or showing an error? Mac finds the fault and tells you what it’ll take to fix.

  • Not cooling or heating properly
  • Water leaking inside
  • Noises and rattles
  • Error codes and flashing lights
  • Tripping the power

Servicing

A service keeps the unit running efficiently and catches small problems before they turn into a breakdown in the middle of summer.

  • Usually 30 minutes to an hour
  • Filters and coils cleaned
  • Drainage checked
  • Electrical connections tested

Every brand

Mac works on all the common split system brands, so there’s no need to hunt down a brand-specific technician.

  • Split systems of every make
  • Older and newer units
  • Home and small commercial
Repair or replace?

A straight answer, on site.

Whether an old unit is worth fixing depends on how old it is and what’s wrong with it. Mac checks it properly first and tells you honestly — repair it if that’s the sensible call, replace it only if it isn’t worth the money.

The first hot week of summer, everyone’s unit gets switched on and every trade on the coast is booked out. If yours struggled last year, get it looked at now.
