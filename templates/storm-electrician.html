Powerlines down?

Keep well back and call Essential Energy on 13 20 80. The street lines, the pole and the line to your house belong to the network — no electrician can touch them until they’ve been made safe.

For trees down, roof damage or flooding, call the SES on 132 500. If anyone’s in danger or there’s a fire, call 000 first.

After the storm

Your side of the meter.

Water in the switchboard or wiring

Stay clear, don’t reset anything, and call. Wet wiring has to be tested before the power goes back on.

Only your place is out

Or a safety switch keeps tripping. When the street has power and you don’t, the fault is usually inside your property.

Generator inlet

An inlet with a changeover switch lets you run the house, or the circuits that matter, safely off a generator through a long outage.

Safety switches & board upgrades

Checking every circuit is properly protected before you need it to be.

Never run a generator through a power point.

Back-feeding through a power point can put line workers in danger and damage the wiring and the generator. The safe way is a generator inlet with a changeover switch, installed by a licensed electrician.
