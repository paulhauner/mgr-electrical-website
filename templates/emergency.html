Safety first

If someone has had a shock, or there’s fire or smoke, call 000 first. Mac can make the property safe and handle the repairs afterwards.

Ring straight away

When it can’t wait.

Burning smells & hot fittings

A switch, power point or light that smells hot or feels warm. If it’s safe, switch that circuit off at the board, then call.

Total or partial power loss

Especially if the neighbours still have power — that usually means the fault is on your side of the meter.

Safety switch keeps tripping

It’s doing its job. Something on that circuit is faulty, and the cause needs finding before it’s reset again.

Storm or water damage

Water near the switchboard or wiring. Keep clear and call before touching anything. More on storm damage →

While you wait

  • Switch off the affected circuit, if it’s safe to
  • Unplug appliances on that circuit
  • Check whether the neighbours have power
  • Keep everyone away from water
  • Don’t open up the switchboard
